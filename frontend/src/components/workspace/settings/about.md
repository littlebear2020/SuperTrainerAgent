# 🤖 About Trainer AI

> **AI-Powered Training Content Creation**

**Trainer AI** is an AI-powered platform for creating professional training content — from course materials and presentations to videos and knowledge cards. Built on the [DeerFlow](https://github.com/bytedance/deer-flow) open-source harness, it combines sandbox execution, persistent memory, and extensible skills to handle tasks that take minutes to hours.

---

## 🌟 Open Source Foundation

Trainer AI is built on [DeerFlow](https://github.com/bytedance/deer-flow), an open-source SuperAgent harness developed by ByteDance.
